The treasurer's books, kept properly — and kept by you.

PennyWarden is free bookkeeping for clubs, booster groups, associations and other small organizations that keep a checkbook, a budget and an annual audit. It runs on your own Windows PC, Mac or Linux server. Your data stays in one folder you control — no subscription, no cloud account.

New in 2.1 Fundraiser reports for your committee — a stakeholder report and a slide deck, the same event across years — a Budget Review and Planning Sheet for next year's budget, and the optional Donations module for donation receipt letters.

What did we plan?

Income and expense budgets for each Fiscal Year, with sub-budgets where you need detail, approved and locked with the year.

What actually happened?

One register per bank account. Split a check across budgets, record transfers once, and see the bank's balance next to everything you have written.

Can we prove it?

Attach the receipt to each transaction. One printable audit report holds the budgets and every transaction followed by its documents.

Since 2.0

Payments: from the bill to the printed check

Enter a bill once — the payee, one line per invoice with its budget and documents — and PennyWarden records the withdrawal in the register and prints the check on your own printer, on the check stock your bank already numbered.

  • Your check stock. Three checks per page or a voucher check with two stubs listing the invoices paid. Adjust every field with a preview drawn to scale, and a printer setup assistant if your printer shifts the page.
  • Signatures under control. Signature images are stored encrypted; a style can have two signature lines, and above an amount you choose the check is left for signing by hand.
  • Nothing goes missing. You confirm the number of the check in the printer; a spoiled check becomes a $0.00 VOID record, and a copy of every printed check is attached to its transaction.
  • Letters and envelopes too. A cover letter listing the invoices — with your logo or on your letterhead — and a #10 envelope, printed right after the check. Checks written by hand are recorded the same way.

The Payments module is optional: an Administrator turns it on in System/About. How check printing works.

Made for the way volunteer treasurers work

Budgets by Fiscal Year

Every budget shows the plan, the actual amounts per quarter and what remains. Changing an approved budget means unlocking it with a reason, which is recorded. Budgets keep their history from one year to the next.

Bank registers

Checks and deposits, split across budgets, with cleared dates and the bank reconciliation. Duplicate entries and check numbers are caught; a mistake is voided, never silently deleted.

Audit-ready reports

The End of Year Audit report, the Fiscal Year Close report and the Financial Flow Report for your board: printable PDFs, built from what is already in the books.

Fundraisers, donations and reminders

Follow an event from the first poster to the last deposit, with a printable cash count sheet and a report for the committee. Thank donors with a receipt letter printed from the deposit. Reminders — once or repeating — keep the treasurer's duties from being forgotten when the treasurer changes.

  • Your data, your computer. One folder, encrypted backups you can schedule, moves between Windows, Mac and Linux.
  • Safe by default. Encrypted bank account numbers, two-step sign-in, and an audit log of every change in plain words, exportable for your auditors.
  • One person or a committee. Run it alone on a laptop, or for several people on a Linux or Windows server with separate roles — installed with one command on either.
  • Easy to look after. Administrators get a security overview, signed-in sessions, system health, maintenance mode and settings that can be changed in the application.
  • Free and open. Licensed under the AGPL-3.0; the source code is on GitHub.

“Windows protected your PC”?

PennyWarden is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. Check the download against the checksum below if you want to be sure it is the original file.

“PennyWarden” Not Opened?

The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS refuses it the first time. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Step by step with pictures.

Try the demo

See PennyWarden with a made-up band boosters club already in it: this year's approved budget, a register with cleared and uncleared items, receipts, a fundraiser, reminders, printed checks and last year closed. Everything you change is undone when PennyWarden restarts, and you can remove the demo at any time to set up your own organization.

  1. Install PennyWarden on your computer (Windows or Mac above).
  2. Download the demo: Demo data (.fmbak)
  3. On the first screen choose Restore from a backup instead, pick the file and enter the passphrase pennywarden demo.
  4. Sign in with the password Demo-PennyWarden-2026 as:
    • demo-admin — Administrator: users, backups, settings (no financial records);
    • demo-budget — Budget Manager: Fiscal Years, budgets, bank accounts, reports;
    • demo-register — Register User: the register, payments and checks;
    • demo-auditor — Auditor: reads everything, including the audit log.

The demo runs only on your own computer (its passwords and key are published). The banner has Reset demo data and Remove the demo and start fresh. More about the demo.
