# What's new in 1.10 — quick guide

*1.10.0: the product page pennywarden.org, a notice when a new version is out, and a fix for typing in dialogs.*

## 1.10.0: pennywarden.org
PennyWarden has its own web page: **<https://pennywarden.org>**. It explains what PennyWarden is and offers the
download for Windows, Mac and Linux directly — the one for the visitor's computer is highlighted — with help for the
"unverified app" warnings. Point new users, your board or your successor there.

## 1.10.0: "a new version is available"
When a newer version of PennyWarden is out, a **gold circle with an up arrow** appears next to your name at the top
right. Click it to read **What's new** — the notes of every release newer than yours, newest first — and to open the
download page. Installing the update is still up to you, as before; your data is kept.

- On a **server**, Administrators also see a banner at the top of every page.
- On a **local install**, the banner is on **My account**.
- PennyWarden checks about once a day. Nothing about your organization is sent.
- Administrators can turn the check off: **System/About → Update check**. **Check now** checks at once.

## 1.10.0: fix
- In the Financial Flow Report, the reason for excluding a line can be typed normally again (the box lost the
  focus after every letter in 1.8.0 and 1.9.0).
