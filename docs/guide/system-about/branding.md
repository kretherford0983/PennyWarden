**System/About → Branding** sets a light and a dark palette, logos and footer text for reports and presentations.
Choose which theme printed reports and presentations use. PennyWarden checks the contrast of each palette so text stays
readable, and presentations also adjust chart colors that would disappear into the slide.

**Logos**: the full logo, the small mark and their dark-background versions can each use the
[organization logo](organization-logos.md), a logo uploaded here, or none. The sample page beside the settings shows
what will print.
