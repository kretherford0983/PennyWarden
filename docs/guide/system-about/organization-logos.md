**System/About → General → Organization logos** (Administrators) keeps your logo in one place, so you upload it once:

- **Logo** — for letters, envelopes, the invoice PDF and report headers.
- **Small mark** (optional) — for report and slide footers.
- **Logo on dark backgrounds** and **Small mark on dark backgrounds** (optional) — for dark slides and pages.

PNG or JPEG, at most 2 MB. Under each logo you see where it is used.

## Choosing the logo in each place
Every place that prints a logo has a **Logo** choice:

- **Organization logo** — prints the logo from this page. Replace it here and every such place prints the new one.
- **Its own logo** — upload a different logo just for that place.
- **No logo**.

The places are each [cover letter](../payments/cover-letters.md), each [envelope](../envelopes-and-signers/envelopes.md),
[the invoice PDF](../invoices/invoice-pdf.md), the donation [receipt letter and year-end statement](../donations/donations-setup.md)
and the four logos of [Branding](branding.md). New templates start with **No logo**, so nothing changes on paper until
you choose.

## Removing a logo
**Remove** lists the places that use it and asks you to confirm. Those places then print no logo until you add a new
one here; their choice stays *Organization logo*. Printed copies already kept (sent invoices, receipts, letters) never
change.

After the upgrade to 2.4, Branding's logos became the organization logos, and a cover letter or invoice PDF that had
the same logo uses them. Nothing prints differently.
