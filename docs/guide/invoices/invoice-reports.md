**Reports → Invoices**:
- **Open invoices** and **Aging** — what is still owed and for how long.
- **Customer statement** — one customer's invoices and payments.
- **Sales tax collected** — per tax rate: taxable sales, tax charged and tax collected in a period.
- **Invoice register** — every invoice in a period.
- **Sales by product / category**.
- **Payments, write-offs, discounts and refunds**.

Overdue invoices show on the Dashboard and as **Invoice reminders**.
