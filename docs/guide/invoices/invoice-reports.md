**Reports → Invoices**:
- **Open invoices** and **Aging** — what is still owed and for how long.
- **Customer statement** — one customer's invoices and payments.
- **Sales tax collected** — per tax rate: taxable sales, tax charged and tax collected in a period.
- **Invoice register** — every invoice in a period.
- **Sales by product / category**.
- **Payments, write-offs, discounts and refunds**.

Overdue invoices show on the Dashboard and as **Invoice reminders**.

## Reports for the board and members

Two invoice reports are made for publishing — a PDF report and a presentation with charts — and are listed under
**Reports → Share with the board and members** (and under Invoices when the catalog is grouped by subject):

- **Sales by product & category** — the period's sales by category, by product within each category, and by Income
  budget: quantity, amount, share of the total, what has been collected so far, and last year beside them.
- **Sales overview** — the period's invoicing in one report: sales and sales tax, money collected and refunded by
  month, what is open and overdue at the end, discounts and write-offs, the top customers and products, and sales tax by
  rate, with last year beside them.

Both count sales as **invoiced** (after discounts, before sales tax), with what has been **collected** beside it.

To run one:
1. Choose the period: **A Fiscal Year quarter or the year** (the same period last year is shown beside it when there
   is an earlier Fiscal Year), or **Dates of my choice** (no comparison). A period that hasn't ended yet is reported as
   of today and marked **Preliminary**.
2. **Show customer names** is on by default. Turn it off to print *Customer 1*, *Customer 2*, … instead.
3. Optionally write a **Commentary** (up to 2,000 characters). It is printed on the first page and gets its own slide in
   the presentation. It is not remembered: write it for each run.
4. **Open** or **Download PDF**, or **Download PPTX** for the presentation.

Anyone who can see invoices can run them (Invoice Managers, Budget Managers, Budget Users, Register Users, Auditors);
Administrators can't. Each run is recorded in the audit log.
