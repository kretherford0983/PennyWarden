1. On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your username.
3. Answer one of your three **security questions**. Case, spaces and punctuation don't matter.
4. On a server, or when you use two-step verification, also enter the code from your authenticator app.
5. Choose a new password and type it again.

You are signed out everywhere else. Wrong answers count like wrong passwords (see [Signing in](sign-in.md)).

**Lost your phone as well?** Use a recovery code instead of the authenticator code. Without a phone and without
recovery codes, ask an Administrator to reset your two-step verification
([Managing users](../users/manage-users.md)).
