PennyWarden keeps the books of a small organization — a club, a booster group, a volunteer association — the way an
auditor would want to see them: budgets planned by one person, money recorded by another, and everything reviewable by
someone else. Nothing financial is ever erased.

**How this guide works**
- The **Contents** page lists the chapters. Each chapter is one area of PennyWarden (the Register, Budgets, Invoices…)
  and lists its pages. Each page explains one task, step by step.
- **Search** at the top of every page finds pages by their words.
- Inside PennyWarden, **Help** in the menu opens this guide, and the **?** button at the top right opens the page about
  the screen you are on. The guide is part of the app, so it works without an internet connection.
- Each page says which version it **applies to**. On [pennywarden.org](https://pennywarden.org/guide/) you can switch to
  the guide of another version.
