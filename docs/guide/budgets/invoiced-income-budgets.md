With the [Invoices](../invoices/about-invoices.md) module on, money collected on invoices goes to **Invoiced Income**
budgets.

1. On **Budgets**, open the row menu (**⋯**) of an Income budget.
2. Choose **Mark as Invoiced Income**. (An approved budget must be unlocked first.)

Its sub-budgets carry the mark, it shows **🧾 Invoiced income**, and copying it into a new year keeps the mark. Invoice
Managers then choose these budgets for products, categories and tax rates; they see only their code and name.
