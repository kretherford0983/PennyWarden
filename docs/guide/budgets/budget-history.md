In **Edit**, **Continues** links a budget to the one it carries on from an earlier year (copied budgets are linked for
you). **History** next to its name lists every linked year: code, name, amount and actual.

**Years before PennyWarden**
1. Open a budget's **History → Add an earlier year**.
2. Enter what was budgeted and actually spent or received. Tick **Estimated** with a note if it is an estimate, or
   choose **Its money was included in another budget that year**.

**Budgets → Before PennyWarden…** lists and links these older budgets. **Import earlier years…** takes a CSV for many
years at once: **Download the template**, fill it in, then **Preview** and **Import**. **Correct** and **Retire** fix
entries with a reason; nothing is deleted. These years show in the History and the Budget Review, never in actuals or
the registers.
