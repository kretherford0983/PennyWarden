- **New budget**, **Edit** (the pencil) and sub-budgets — while the budget is Draft or unlocked.
- **Unlock** an approved budget to amend it: a reason is required and recorded. **Lock** it again before closing the
  year.
- **Reject** a budget: its allowed amount becomes zero, its existing transactions stay, and no new ones can be allocated
  to it.
- **Inactivate** a budget you no longer use; it stays in history.
- **Delete budget…** (Budget Admin only) removes a budget of a year that is not approved yet — for example one copied by
  mistake — if it has no transactions or sub-budgets. A reason is required; Auditors still see it as *Deleted*.
- Click a budget's code or name to open the Register filtered to that budget.
