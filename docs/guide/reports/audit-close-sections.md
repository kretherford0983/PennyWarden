The End of Year Audit and Fiscal Year Close reports trace every dollar back to the register. When the year has
**events**, **invoices** or **donations**, each gets a section of its own after the transactions (in that order, before
the signature page).

## When a section is included

- **Always, when the Fiscal Year has that module's records.** There's no option to leave a section out: an audit
  should show everything.
- **Even if the module has since been turned off.** Turning a module off doesn't remove a year's records from its
  report. The section says the module is currently off, and so does the *Module sections* line on the Fiscal Year
  Review page.
- **Never for a module that has no records in the year**, so an organization that doesn't use invoices sees no
  invoice pages.

Each section sits between a **Start of …** page and an **End of …** page that state its page range. Every page in
between shows *Invoices – section page k of n* (or *Donations*, *Fundraisers*), so a reviewer can tell no page is
missing.

## Tracing both ways

- In a section, every payment or gift has a **Transaction** number and a **Page**: the page of the same report where
  that transaction and its receipts are printed. *Not in this report* means the transaction is in another Fiscal
  Year's report, or in an account a single-account report leaves out.
- On each transaction's page, a **Linked to** line names the invoice or donation that money belongs to, for example
  *Invoice INV-2026-0012 (payment)* or *Donation #7 — Pat Sample, receipt R-2026-0004*.

These sections are for tracing. Income is counted once, when it's deposited: the amounts in a section are **not** a
second income total and aren't added to the budget figures.

## The Invoices section

- **The year's invoices:** every invoice dated in the Fiscal Year, plus any older invoice that had a payment, refund,
  credit, write-off or void during it. Each shows its customer, dates, total and current status, then its activity in
  the year:
  - payments, overpayments, refunds of an overpayment, and payments made from a customer's credit;
  - a write-off, with who asked for it, the Budget Manager who approved it (and *self-approved* when it was the same
    person) and the reason;
  - a void, with who voided it and why.
  - **Open at the year's end** is the total less the payments dated on or before the year's last day (zero for a
    draft, or an invoice voided or written off by then).
- **A payment counts in the year of its deposit.** An invoice dated in December and paid in February appears in both
  years' reports: the first shows it open at the year's end, the second shows the payment.
- **Invoice numbers:** an invoice's number is taken when it's created and never reused, so every number from the
  year's first to its last should be explained. The report lists the voided ones (with the reason), drafts never sent,
  numbers that belong to an invoice dated in another year, and any number with no invoice at all (which should never
  happen; look into it if it does).
- **Invoiced Income tie-out:** the Invoiced Income budgets' actual for the year (deposits less refunds) compared with
  the part of it that's linked to invoices. The difference is shown, and every register line on an Invoiced Income
  budget that has no invoice link is listed, for example a deposit entered while the Invoices module was off. Amounts
  an invoice payment put on other budgets, such as sales tax, are listed below for reference.
- **Open invoices at the year's end:** each invoice with a balance on the last day, as before. Open invoices don't
  stop a Fiscal Year from closing.

## The Donations section

- **The year's donations:** each donation with money deposited in the year, an item received in it, a receipt printed
  in it, or cancelled in it. It shows the donor, its status, the receipt in force, and each line:
  - money, with the deposit's transaction and page;
  - items, with the organization's own estimate (or why there's none). Items aren't in the register.
  - the purpose, and any goods or services given in exchange (as printed on the receipt).
- **Cancelled donations** show who cancelled them and why.
- **Receipts printed:** every receipt printed in the year — originals, duplicates (same number) and corrected receipts
  with the number they replace — and a check that every receipt number from the first to the last is accounted for.
- **Money awaiting a deposit at the year's end:** money donations recorded by then and still not linked to a deposit.

## A single-account Audit report

When the Audit report is run for one bank account, the Invoices and Donations sections show only the invoices and
donations with money deposited to that account in the year. The number checks, the tie-out, the open invoices, the
receipts list and the cancelled or awaiting donations are in the all-accounts report.
