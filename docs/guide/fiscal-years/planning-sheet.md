**Planning sheet…** (on the Fiscal Year page and in Reports) prints the form for the budget planning meeting: previous
budgeted amounts, actual year-to-date, projections and a blank *Proposed* column, with blank rows for new budgets.

- If the next year already exists as a Draft, its amounts print as *Proposed* with a blank *Proposed Revision* column.
- Tick **Include the Budget Review** to add the review to the same PDF.

After the meeting, create the next year with **Copy budgets from** ([Creating a Fiscal Year](create-fiscal-year.md))
and adjust the amounts.
