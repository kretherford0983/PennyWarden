**Close Fiscal Year…** is refused while:
- the year is not Approved;
- any budget is unlocked;
- any transaction in the year has not cleared;
- a Fiscal Year review item is unresolved ([Reviews and missing checks](../register/reviews-and-missing-checks.md));
- an allocation is invalid or incomplete;
- there is no **Audit Signoff** document.

Warnings — rejected-budget activity, expense budgets over budget, reviewed cross-year allocations, voided transactions,
missing checks, missing documentation, open invoices — are listed for you to read but don't stop the close.

1. Choose **Close Fiscal Year…**.
2. Read the warnings.
3. Confirm that the year has been reviewed and approved for closure.

A closed year can't be reopened; its budgets and allocations can no longer change, and a copy of the **Fiscal Year
Close report** is kept on its page.
