**Budget proposal** (Reports → Plan budgets; Budget Managers) presents a Fiscal Year's budgets for approval — to the
board, the officers or the members — as a PDF report and a presentation (`.pptx`).

- Choose a **Draft** year (titled *Proposed*) or an **Approved** one (titled *Approved*). Closed years are not offered.
  Rejected budgets are left out.
- Each budget line shows:
  - **last year's budget**;
  - **last year's actual** — while last year is still running it is *projected* to the year end: the activity so far,
    averaged per month and carried to the end of the year, the same projection as the Budget Review (your Budget Review
    projection choices apply; in its first month the year to date is shown instead);
  - the **proposed** (or approved) amount;
  - the **change**: the proposed amount minus last year's budget, in dollars and as a percentage of last year's budget.
- Lines are matched to last year through their *continues from* link, else by the same code, so a renamed or
  renumbered budget stays one line. A **new** budget shows "—" for last year. A budget of last year that has **no line
  this year** is listed as *Not in* the new year (for example *Not in FY2028*), so approvers see what was dropped.
- The **totals** are each year's own totals: last year's budget and actual over all of last year's budgets, the
  proposed total over this year's.
- The presentation opens with a summary — proposed income, expense and net, each with last year's budget — then the
  Income and Expense tables. An optional **commentary** goes under the summary and into the speaker notes.

**One deck for the year-end meeting:** in **Quarterly / Annual**, choose **Annual** and tick **Add the … budget
proposal** (named for next year). The proposal follows the year's financial review in the same PDF and the same presentation (before
the closing slide). The option is offered to Budget Managers once the next Fiscal Year exists (Draft is enough).
