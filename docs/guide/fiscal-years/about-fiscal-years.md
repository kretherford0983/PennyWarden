A Fiscal Year is the period your budgets cover. Its status is **Draft**, **Approved** or **Closed**.
- A **Draft** year is fully usable: it appears everywhere, takes budgets and takes transactions.
- **Approval** locks its budgets ([Approving a Fiscal Year](approve-fiscal-year.md)).
- **Closing** makes it permanent ([Closing a Fiscal Year](close-fiscal-year.md)).

Quarters run three months at a time from the start date: a year starting July 1 has Q1 Jul–Sep, Q2 Oct–Dec, Q3 Jan–Mar
and Q4 Apr–Jun.
