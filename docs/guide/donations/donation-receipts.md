1. On a deposit in the register choose **Donation receipt…**.
2. Tick the lines that are donations; add the purpose and any goods or services received.
3. **Save donation lines**, **Preview**, then **Print receipt**.

Receipts are numbered per year (R-2026-0001) and attached to the deposit; reprints are **Duplicate** or **Corrected**.
After printing, the **Envelope** step prints the donor's envelope.

**Donations → New donation** records a pledge or gift before the money arrives and prints its receipt; **Create
transaction** later makes the deposit. Tick **Item (not money)** for physical gifts.
