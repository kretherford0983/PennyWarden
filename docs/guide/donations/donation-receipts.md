1. On a deposit in the register choose **Donation receipt…**.
2. Tick the lines that are donations; add the purpose and any goods or services received.
3. **Save donation lines**, **Preview**, then **Print receipt**.

Receipts are numbered per year (R-2026-0001) and attached to the deposit; reprints are **Duplicate** or **Corrected**.
After printing, the **Envelope** step prints the donor's envelope.

**Donations → New donation** records a pledge or gift before the money arrives and prints its receipt; **Create
transaction** later makes the deposit. Tick **Item (not money)** for physical gifts.

## When the deposit is already in the register

If the money was deposited and entered in the register first, link it to the donation instead of ticking it as a new
one (which would give the donor a second receipt for the same gift):

- **From the donation:** open it on the Donations page and choose **Link a deposit…** on its line. The list shows the
  donor's lines on active deposits (not in a Closed Fiscal Year). Pick one and **Link**. If the deposit was entered
  under another entity, correct the line's entity in the register first.
- **From the deposit:** in **Donation receipt…**, tick the line. When the donor has a donation awaiting a deposit, a
  choice appears under the donor: **New donation** or **Donation #N (awaiting deposit…)**. It starts on the awaiting
  donation when exactly one has the same amount; otherwise choose it yourself. **Save** links them.

Once linked, the donation follows the deposit: it takes the deposit line's amount (you are asked to confirm when the
amounts differ, and its next receipt is a corrected one), its date received is kept unless it is after the deposit,
and its printed receipts are attached to the deposit. If the line was already ticked as a donation with its own
receipt, you are asked to confirm; that receipt becomes **Void** and its donation shows as Cancelled, *Replaced by
donation #N*.

## Emailing a receipt

When the mail server is set up (System/About → Email, the same one Invoices use), the receipt screens show **Email…**
(**Email receipt…** on the Donations page) next to **Print receipt**; without it, no Email button shows.

**Email…** opens the email with the donor's address, and the subject and message from Donations Setup — change any of
them before sending. Sending **issues** the receipt exactly as printing does (its number; original, duplicate or
corrected), so it counts as given and isn't printed twice by mistake. If the mail server refuses the email, nothing is
issued and the receipt can be printed or emailed again. Every email is in the email log.
