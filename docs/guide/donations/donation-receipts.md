1. On a deposit in the register choose **Donation receipt…**.
2. Tick the lines that are donations; add the purpose and any goods or services received.
3. **Save donation lines**, **Preview**, then **Print receipt**.

Receipts are numbered per year (R-2026-0001) and attached to the deposit; reprints are **Duplicate** or **Corrected**.
After printing, the **Envelope** step prints the donor's envelope.

**Donations → New donation** records a pledge or gift before the money arrives and prints its receipt; **Create
transaction** later makes the deposit. Tick **Item (not money)** for physical gifts.

## Emailing a receipt

When the mail server is set up (System/About → Email, the same one Invoices use), the receipt screens show **Email…**
(**Email receipt…** on the Donations page) next to **Print receipt**; without it, no Email button shows.

**Email…** opens the email with the donor's address, and the subject and message from Donations Setup — change any of
them before sending. Sending **issues** the receipt exactly as printing does (its number; original, duplicate or
corrected), so it counts as given and isn't printed twice by mistake. If the mail server refuses the email, nothing is
issued and the receipt can be printed or emailed again. Every email is in the email log.
