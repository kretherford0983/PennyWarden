An optional module. An Administrator turns it on and enters the organization's **EIN** and **address** in
[System/About → General](../system-about/optional-modules.md).

**Donations Setup** has the receipt letter and the year-end statement letter with `{VARIABLES}` (type `{` for the
list), the signer, **Include EIN on receipts**, the window-envelope layout and fold marks.

**Logo**: the receipt letter prints the [organization logo](../system-about/organization-logos.md), its own logo, or
none, at the **Top left**, **Top center**, **Top right, above the organization's name** (the letter text moves
down) or **Top right, beside the organization's name** (the letter text stays where it is) — the same choices as a
payment cover letter. With a window envelope it prints at the top right, clear of the windows. The year-end
statement uses the same as the receipt letter, or its own choice.
