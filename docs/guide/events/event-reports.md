- **Report…** at the top of the event's page: the stakeholder **Financial & Proceeds Statement** as PDF or Word, or a
  **Presentation** (`.pptx`), with options for itemized revenue, contributor names and year over year. Presentation
  charts keep their text off the bars and lines and adjust any color too close to the slide background, so they stay
  readable in every color scheme.
- **Fiscal Year report…** on the Events page: the year's fundraising totals and trends (Events are listed separately).
- **Fundraiser series** lets you rename series, enter prior years from before PennyWarden (optionally **Estimated**),
  correct, retire and merge them.
