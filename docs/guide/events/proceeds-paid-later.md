Some events are run with an outside organization that pays its share later — a county fair in August whose fair board
sends the check the next April, in the next Fiscal Year. PennyWarden keeps the cash where it arrived and still shows
the money with the event.

**Setting it up** (Budget Managers): in the event's **Edit** form tick **Proceeds paid later by an outside
organization**, optionally with the **expected payer** (an entity) and the **expected month**. **Create next year's…**
copies the flag and the payer. No budget of next year is needed — next year's budgets usually don't exist yet.

**While you wait** the event is **Awaiting proceeds**:
- it is listed under **Attention** on the Dashboard, and its page and list row say *Proceeds pending*;
- its reports show *Proceeds pending*, with the expected month and payer, instead of a finished result;
- its results are never *Final* — not even when its Fiscal Years close — and **Mark results final** waits;
- waiting never holds up closing a Fiscal Year.

**When the check comes:**
1. Enter it in the Register as a normal deposit, dated the day it arrived, against any Income budget of that year
   (`Other` too). If it comes from the expected payer, PennyWarden asks *Is this the proceeds for …?* — **Yes** links it.
2. Or, on the event's page, **Add received proceeds…** lists deposit lines dated on or after the event (the expected
   payer's first) — **Link** the right one. Several deposits can be linked (installments).
3. When nothing more is expected, click **All proceeds received**. If nothing will ever come, a Budget Manager clicks
   **No proceeds received…** and says why. **Awaiting again** reverses either.

**How it counts:**
- *Cash basis*: the deposit stays in the Fiscal Year it was received and in that year's budget actuals.
- *With the event*: the linked deposit counts in the event's income and net, and in its own year in the series and the
  Fiscal Year fundraising report (the 2025 fair shows its full result in 2025).
- *Once*: a linked deposit counts for this event only — another event using the same budget and filter leaves it out.
- The Fiscal Year fundraising report of the year the money arrived lists it under **Proceeds received this year for
  earlier events**, and the Audit / Close reports of that year include the event.

**Unlinking** (with an optional reason) and the outcome are audited. In a Closed year only a Budget Manager can link or
unlink — it is reporting only: no money changes.

**Past years:** a Budget Manager can set up an event dated in a Closed year, with that year's budgets, to load history
(reporting only; its lines can't be put in buckets or excluded). For years before PennyWarden, enter results by hand in
**Series** — enter each fair in its own year with the amount received for it.
