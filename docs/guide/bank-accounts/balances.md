For a register account PennyWarden works the balance out from the opening balance and the register:
- **Current balance** — only what has **cleared**, i.e. what the bank has. Used on the Dashboard and this page.
- **Available balance** — everything written or deposited, cleared or not. Shown in the Register.

For an account without a register (investments, CDs, cash):
1. Choose **Update balance**.
2. Enter the balance and its **As of** date, e.g. a statement date.

Each update is kept; **History** lists them all. In the history's **⋯** menu, **Correct…** fixes an amount or date and
**Retire…** takes a wrong entry out — both with a reason. Balances dated before your first Fiscal Year are marked
*Entered*. In a closed Fiscal Year only a Budget Admin can change a balance, and the year then shows **Balance changed
after close**.
