- **Fiscal Year reviews** (Register) lists allocations whose budget year differs from the transaction date's year, or
  that had no year. **Mark reviewed** (with a note) confirms one is intentional; reviewed ones remain valid. Unresolved
  reviews block closing that year.
- **Possible missing checks** lists numbers skipped in the account's sequence: **Enter transaction**, **Record as VOID
  check** or **Confirm not missing…**.
