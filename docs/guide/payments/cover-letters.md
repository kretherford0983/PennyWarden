A cover letter lists the invoices a check pays. In **Payments Setup → Documents**:
1. Create or edit a letter. Its live preview uses sample data.
2. **Letterhead** lines (one per row, `{VARIABLES}` suggested as you type), the subject, opening, the invoice table's
   columns, the closing and who signs.
3. **Logo**: the [organization logo](../system-about/organization-logos.md), **Its own logo** (add a PNG or JPEG) or
   **No logo**, and where it goes: **Top left**, **Top center** (the letterhead lines are centered too), **Top
   right, above the letterhead** (the letter text moves down) or **Top right, beside the letterhead** (level with the
   organization's name; the letter text stays where it is).
4. **Pre-printed letterhead paper** keeps the top of the page blank instead.
5. **Window envelope** places the addresses for a #10 double-window envelope; **Fold marks** prints small ticks where
   the page folds.
