1. **Payments → New payment**, or **Create payment…** on a withdrawal already in the register.
2. Enter the payee, the invoices being paid (one line per invoice: budget, invoice number, description, amount) and the
   date. The check amount is the total.
3. Choose **Continue to printing**. Choose the check style, the signer and whether to print the **cover letter** and the
   **envelope**.
4. Print, then tell PennyWarden how it went: **Yes – done**, **Reprint** or **Mark spoiled** (the number becomes a
   $0.00 VOID record).

**I'm writing the check by hand** records a handwritten check; an electronic payment is saved without printing.
